Sellers

This application uses a third-party product API (TP Gifts Store) as the supplier. Seller/marketplace functionality is not applicable to the current business model. Supplier management is done via the API Settings panel.

Reviews

Product review functionality can be added in a future update. Reviews from the TP Gifts Store API are surfaced through the product catalogue.

Categories

Product categories are sourced from the TP Gifts Store API. Category management happens on the supplier platform. You can configure markup by category in Settings → Pricing.

Seller Payouts

This store uses a single-seller (owner) model. Settlement status is tracked per transaction. See Transactions for payment settlement details. Configure payment gateway settlement in Settings → Payment Gateway.

Pricing / Profit Margin

Configure your markup over the TP Gifts Store supplier cost.

Example: 10 for 10% markup. Provider cost ₦10,000 → Customer price ₦11,000
Payscribe typically charges a percentage fee for local cards. This is deducted from gross profit to show net profit.

Profit Calculation Example

Payment Gateway — Payscribe

Payscribe handles customer payments. Keys are stored server-side only.

Never exposed to the browser. Used server-side for payment verification.

Settlement Architecture

Customer payments settle to your Payscribe merchant account. Payscribe batches and settles funds to your bank account per your configured settlement schedule. Net profit per transaction is tracked in the Transactions page. Configure your bank account and settlement schedule in your Payscribe Dashboard → Settlement.
